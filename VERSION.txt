Team_calculator v23.4.25
Release: team_calculator_v23_4_25_other_sprints_filter_deploy
Base version: v23.4.24 completed_task_pool_filter_deploy

Changes:
- Added the "Задачи других спринтов" filter to "Общий пул оценённых задач".
- "Готовы к планированию" now contains only estimated, unfinished tasks that are not linked to any sprint.
- "Добавлены в текущий спринт" remains limited to unfinished tasks in the currently opened sprint.
- "Задачи других спринтов" contains unfinished tasks linked to a different sprint.
- Completed tasks remain available only through "Выполненные" and "Все задачи".
- The task pool summary now shows current-sprint and other-sprint counts separately.

Deployment:
- Application change: index.html
- Firestore Rules change for this feature: not required
- Included firestore.rules: current v23.4.23 rules supplied by the product owner
